FAQ

Common questions from clients

How long does a website take?

Starter: 7 days. Business: 10 days. E-commerce: 14 days. Timeline starts after content (text, images) is received.

Do you handle domain and hosting?

Yes. Domain registration, hosting setup, SSL, and email configuration are included. No extra cost.

What if I need changes after launch?

Starter includes 1 month free support. Business includes 3 months. After that, ₹2,000/month for unlimited updates.

Can I see a demo before committing?

Yes. I'll design a free homepage mockup in Elementor. If you like it, we proceed. If not, no obligation.

Will my site work on mobile?

Every site is mobile-first. Tested on iOS Safari, Chrome Android, and tablet breakpoints before delivery.

Do you build on WordPress?

Yes. WordPress + Elementor. Easy for you to edit text/images later without code. No vendor lock-in.
